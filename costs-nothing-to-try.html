Why it replays a whole session, not one request

The recording seam is the framework's own LLMInput/LLMOutput types, not HTTP and not a provider SDK. Three things follow, and the third is the one that makes it more than a test fixture.

  • It captures tool calls too, so an agentic loop that reads files and runs a command replays end to end — not just a single completion.
  • A recording is portable across providers. One made against Anthropic replays against a different implementation, because the seam is above the SDK.
  • The debugging story, the testing story and the eval story are one mechanism. The same cassette that makes the suite free is what pack try uses to measure somebody else's extension before you install it.

Guardrails first is a framework invariant rather than a rendering preference, and this list is what the characterization corpus asserts on — so one artifact serves offline inspection and migration equivalence at once.

--dry-run proves the wiring with a canned answer. triage routes to a local model at genuinely $0 while still counting tokens. And pack try measures a third-party extension against a replayed recording before you decide to trust it.

What it does not do

A replay is only as good as what somebody recorded

A cassette holds the answers its author recorded. A case they did not record cannot be run — and the framework reports that as unrecorded rather than as a failure, because an absence of evidence is not evidence of absence. That is the honest answer and it is still an absence.

Somebody has to pay once. Free measurement exists because a real model call was made and recorded. The shipped cassette is one such call against one merged pull request; a pack with no recording cannot be measured for nothing, and the tool says so instead of returning an empty result.

And a $0 trial ranks on deterministic cases only. Rubric cases need a judge, and a judge costs money — so a corpus that is mostly rubrics shows a small decided count beside a large outstanding one. That is the true shape of its evidence, printed on every run.
