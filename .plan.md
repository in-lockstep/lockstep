## Context

The 2026-09-06 completeness audit (fourteen agents over `main` at `539247a`) read the code, tests,
docs and the dogfood ledger against the mission sentence and each objective's own words. The
ledger says 10 of 12 objectives are held. Against the sentences, three hold cleanly (O4, O7, O9);
seven are partial for reasons the ledger does not state, because they live in clauses no gate
covers. The audit filed ten issues, #307 to #316, one per top finding, each with acceptance
criteria. There are no other open issues.

Beyond those ten, the ledger itself records what is left: O2 is partial on `GATE-TOOLING-3`
(seven manifest ecosystems declined rather than read), O5 is partial on `GATE-LEDGER-2` (no
post-merge window comparison), six gates are `deferred` under design §17.11 (`ctx.fan_out` and
`ctx.park` with a `SHARED` ledger store), README carries two `planned` rows (flaky-test adapter,
shared ledger store) and one `partial` (GitLab, never executed), `docs/needs.md` leaves N3 (time
to first value) untimed, N5 (a rubric judge) unbound and N8 (cost per merged change) unpriced,
and 39 held gates are cited by no objective, seven of them the spend-and-time discipline the
mission's "disciplined" clause is about.

This plan closes all ten issues and takes every objective to the most honest terminal state the
evidence supports: `held` where a gate can carry it, or a row whose wording says exactly what
cannot be closed on this repository and why. "Complete" here means checkable, not green.

## Standing rules for every PR

- One PR per item, Conventional Commit subject, `make check` green from a cold `.mypy_cache`,
  falsification controls fired and restored from the commit, `git push --no-follow-tags`.
- A gate is cited in the **name or docstring of a collected test function**, never in a module
  docstring or a comment. Phase 8 changes the ratchet to require that, and any PR that cites a
  gate the old way will be reopened by it.
- A new `held` row is cited by an objective row in the same PR, or the exact
  "N held gates are cited by no objective row" sentence in `design/objectives.md` trips.
- The census sentence ("N of M are `held`") changes only in Phases 7 and 8, always in both
  `CLAUDE.md` and `design/objectives.md`.
- `CLI_HELPER_STATEMENTS` in `tests/in_lockstep/test_layering.py` is re-pinned per PR with the
  argument the comment block already uses (parsing, composing, rendering, translating).
- A person's act sits immediately after the PR that makes it meaningful and before the PR whose
  test reads its result. Acts are the owner's and are listed as such.
