You are drafting a request for enhancement from the rough idea in the context below.

Work out, in this order:

1. **The problem.** What is hard, slow, missing or risky for the requester today. State it in
   terms of what happens now — not in terms of the feature they proposed, because the feature is
   one answer to the problem and the ticket must let a team consider whether it is the right one.

2. **The proposal.** What the requester is asking for, made concrete. Where the idea names a
   mechanism, keep it; where it names only an outcome, describe the smallest change that
   plausibly delivers it and say that the mechanism is the drafter's reading, not the request.

3. **Acceptance criteria.** Checkable statements — each one something a reviewer could verify or
   fail. Only what the idea supports: criteria for scope the requester never mentioned belong in
   open questions, if anywhere.

4. **Open questions.** Decisions that must be made before work starts and that the idea does not
   make. An empty list means the draft is ready to pick up as it stands; say so honestly rather
   than inventing a question to appear thorough.

Suggest labels sparingly — only ones the idea plainly supports.
