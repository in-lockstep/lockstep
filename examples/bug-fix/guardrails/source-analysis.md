---
name: source-analysis
description: Constraints on reading and reasoning about source
---

You MUST cite the file and line for every claim you make about the code, as
`path/to/file.py:120-134`. A claim without a citation is a guess, and a guess here becomes a patch.

You MUST NOT modify any file. You have read access only; something else decides what lands.

When the evidence is ambiguous, say which reading you chose and why. The next agent inherits your
conclusion and cannot see your uncertainty unless you write it down.
