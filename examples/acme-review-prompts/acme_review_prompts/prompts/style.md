name: acme-style
description: How Acme wants a finding phrased. Rides after a shipped body as emphasis, never in place of it.
---
Phrase every finding as the line, then the consequence, then the smallest change that removes it.
Name the SQLAlchemy 2.x construct at fault where one is involved; "session misuse" is not a finding.
Do not propose a new dependency; say what the change would need and stop there.
